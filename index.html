ColorCore KLWP Theme Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ColorCore KLWP Theme ("ColorCore", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

ColorCore does not directly collect, store, sell, or maintain a database of personal information about its users.

ColorCore does not use its own advertising, analytics, user-account, or cloud-storage system.

The ColorCore Android application does not request permissions to directly access your location, contacts, calendar, camera, photos, messages, phone data, or notifications.

Use of Kustom KLWP

ColorCore is a theme designed for use with Kustom KLWP (Kustom Live Wallpaper Maker). The app provides the ColorCore theme and works with Kustom KLWP to display and operate the wallpaper.

Some features of the ColorCore theme may display information that Kustom KLWP is permitted to access on your device. Depending on the features enabled in the theme and the permissions you grant to Kustom KLWP, this may include information such as location, calendar information, contacts, and notifications.

These features are used to provide the wallpaper functionality you have chosen to use. ColorCore does not independently collect this information for its own servers or databases.

Kustom KLWP is a separate application developed by Zavasoft S.R.L. and has its own privacy policy and terms of service. Users should review Kustom's privacy policy to understand how Kustom handles information within its application.

Opening Other Applications

ColorCore's theme may contain buttons that open other applications installed on your device, such as the camera, gallery, phone, messaging, notes, calendar, or other applications.

These buttons are intended to launch the selected application. ColorCore does not need to access the contents of those applications merely because a button can open them.

Notifications

Some ColorCore features may display notification information through Kustom KLWP when notification access has been granted to Kustom KLWP. ColorCore does not operate its own notification collection or notification server.

Location Information

Some ColorCore wallpaper features may use location information through Kustom KLWP, for example to provide location-related information or location-dependent wallpaper functions.

Location access is controlled by Android permissions and the settings of Kustom KLWP. ColorCore does not maintain its own location database or use location information for advertising.

Data Sharing

ColorCore does not sell users' personal information and does not intentionally share users' personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information handled by Kustom KLWP or other third-party applications is subject to those applications' own privacy policies and practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

ColorCore does not maintain user accounts or a server-side database containing personal user information. Because ColorCore does not maintain such personal information on its own servers, there is no ColorCore account or server-side personal-data record that users need to request deletion of.

Information stored or managed by Kustom KLWP or other applications on your device is controlled by those applications and the Android operating system.

Security

ColorCore does not operate a server used to store users' personal information. We nevertheless recommend keeping your Android device and the applications you use with ColorCore up to date and protected with appropriate device security.

Children's Privacy

ColorCore is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality or data practices change. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
